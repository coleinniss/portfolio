About me

My past experiences, I've dabbled in unique projects such as: keyboard accessibility standards, inclusive design methodologies, and user research focused on underserved populations.

These experiences has shaped my perspective on how digital independence directly impacts personal well-being. This further fuels my passion for exploring specific user profiles and understanding their underlying challenges.

I believe that understanding without transactional expectations is essential to creating truly inclusive design solutions. This philosophy drives my commitment to solving complex design problems that is meaningful for the users while also accomplishing business requirements.

Redesigning Connections’ Onboarding Experience

The Connections onboarding redesign addressed critical user retention where 80% of new users abandoned the sobriety support app after first login. The goal was to guide patients from registration to an active community through interactive walkthroughs and as well as add the app’s first motion designs along with implementing a new visual design language
